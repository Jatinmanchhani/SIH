SOP-MECH-014: Flange Bolting and Torque Procedure

1. Scope
This procedure covers bolted flange joint assembly for process piping rated Class 150
through Class 900, excluding gasket-specific low-torque joints covered under SOP-MECH-019.

2. Torque Specification
Standard carbon steel flange bolts (ASTM A193 B7 studs with A194 2H nuts) shall be
torqued in a minimum of three passes using the star (cross) pattern:
  Pass 1: 30% of final target torque
  Pass 2: 60% of final target torque
  Pass 3: 100% of final target torque
Final target torque for 3/4 inch studs on Class 300 flanges is 420 N-m. For 1 inch studs
on Class 600 flanges, final target torque is 610 N-m. Refer to Table 4 of the master
torque chart for all other stud sizes and pressure classes.

3. Lubrication
All studs and nut faces shall be lubricated with an approved anti-seize compound prior
to assembly. Dry torqueing is not permitted; it produces unreliable preload and is a
recurring root cause in the joint leak log.

4. Verification
A calibrated torque wrench (calibration valid within 90 days) is mandatory. Hand-tight
plus impact wrench finishing is not an acceptable substitute for calibrated torque
verification on any Class 300 or higher joint.

5. Documentation
The fitter shall record stud size, target torque, pattern used, and wrench calibration
ID on the joint completion checklist (Form MECH-014-A) before the joint is released for
hydro test.
