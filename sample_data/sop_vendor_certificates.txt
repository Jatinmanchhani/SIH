SOP-PROC-007: Vendor Certificate Validity and Onboarding Exceptions

1. Purpose
Defines the handling of vendor compliance certificates (GST registration, ISO 9001,
material test certificates, welder qualification certificates) during onboarding and
periodic revalidation.

2. Standard Validity Rule
A vendor certificate presented as part of onboarding or a purchase order release must
have at least 30 days of remaining validity from the date of submission. Certificates
expiring within 30 days shall be flagged "conditional" and routed for renewal
confirmation before the vendor is marked active in the vendor master.

3. Expired Certificate Handling
If a vendor certificate has expired at the time of review:
  a. The vendor record shall be held in "on-hold" status; no new purchase orders may
     be released against it.
  b. Procurement shall issue a renewal request to the vendor with a 10 working day
     response window.
  c. If no renewed certificate is received within the window, the vendor is escalated
     to the category manager for a sourcing risk review.
  d. An exception to proceed with an expired certificate requires written sign-off from
     the Procurement Head and is valid for a single purchase order only, not a blanket
     waiver.

4. Documentation Requirement
Every exception approval must be logged in the vendor exception register with: vendor
ID, certificate type, expiry date, business justification, approver name, and the PO
number the exception applies to.
